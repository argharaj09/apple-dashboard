Macro vs micro indicators

Macro: real GDP growth, CPI inflation, unemployment, policy interest rate, exchange rate. Micro: iPhone ASP, units, price elasticity, gross margin, market share, Lerner index.

Fiscal policy: government spending/tax changes (here: stimulus to household income and corporate tax rate). Monetary policy: central-bank rate that changes financing costs for phone buyers.
